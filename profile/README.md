## What we make

We are building a global catalog of practical software, storefront tools, and learning materials. Every product has its own documentation, clear requirements, and release files.

| Scripts & code | Store and web tools | Creative & learning resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PHP, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS projects | E-commerce extensions, themes, templates, and practical utilities | Graphics, downloadable guides, stories, tutorials, and courses |

## Featured work

### [SIMIPAY (M-PESA) for PrestaShop](https://github.com/simiyu-samuel/simipay-prestashop)

An open-source PrestaShop payment module that connects checkout to Kopo Kopo. Its repository includes installation guidance, compatibility notes, and downloadable releases.

## Technology

We choose the tools that fit each product. Our current work includes **PHP, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**; the broader catalog is expanding into **Python and TypeScript** as new products are released.

The SimiForge marketplace is in development with **PHP, Laravel, Livewire, Tailwind CSS, and Vite**, with Paystack checkout integration.

## How we work

- Products are designed to solve a clear, practical need.
- Compatibility, requirements, and installation steps belong in the product documentation.
- Product pages and release notes describe what is available and what has been verified.
- The catalog serves a global audience and is not tied to one platform or country.
